- &emsp;道本連環畫，包括兩個故事，⼀是“⻘年李⻑寬的壁落”⼀是“周眞森怎樣⾛上犯罪道路的”。故事分別描寫了兩個孩⼦，因在舊社會迷戀反動、淫穢、荒誕的⿈⾊書籍，⽽使思想深受毒害，最後，竟⾛上荒淫無恥的腐朽⽣活道路甚⾄被反⾰命分⼦利⽤。通過他們的不同經歷，充分揭露了反動、深穢、荒誕的⿈⾊書籍的罪惡實質。
- &emsp;This lianhuanhua includes two stories. One is “The Degeneration of Young Li Changkuan,” and the other is “How Zhou Zhensen Embarked on a Path of Crime.” Each story portrays a child who was morally poisoned, because of his obsession with reactionary, obscene, and absurd yellow books from old society. In the end, they embarked on a decadent lifepath of licentiousness and shamelessness, and counterrevolutionary elements even took advantage of them. Through their different experiences, this lianhuanhua thoroughly exposes the evil nature of reactionary, licentious, and absurd yellow books.
