- 十四、&emsp;直到偷同學們訂“少年報”的錢時，才被發現；這時已偷過十多次了。當一雙雙斥责的眼睛看着他的時候，堕落顽固的思想支持着他，他並不感到羞慚，反而“理直氣壯”地為自己的可恥行為申辯。
- 14.&emsp;He was discovered only when he stole the money his classmates had collected to subscribe to the “Young People’s Newspaper”; by then, he had already stolen more than ten times. When pairs of reproachful eyes stared at him, sustained by his degenerate and stubborn thinking, he felt no shame at all; instead, he “self-righteously” defended his disgraceful behavior.
