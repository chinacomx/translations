- 三、&emsp;解放後，家庭生活得到了改善，李長寬進了學校；但往日的愛好，仍然使他迷戀着小人書攤。他幼小的心靈裹，已深深染上了“行俠打刼”、“姦婦誘女”的“侠客氣慨”。所以便聚夥打架，偷農民的瓜，還經常欺侮女同學。認為這就是“英雄好漢”。
- 3.&emsp;After liberation, family living conditions improved, and Li Changkuan went to school. However, his former habit still drew him obsessively to the xiaorenshu bookstalls. His childish heart was already deeply tainted with the “chivalrous spirit” of “robbing the rich and plundering” and “seducing and raping women.” So he gathered companions to fight, stole melons from farmers, and often bullied female classmates, believing this to be the behavior of a “true brave hero.”
