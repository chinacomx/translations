- 一、&emsp;李長寬，同所有的孩子一樣，原是一個純潔、可愛的孩子。解放前，父親在南京鐵路上做工，因家窮沒錢上學，使他每天游蕩街頭，和小流氓們混在一起，打架、鬧事；有空就看些荒淫、神怪的小人書，竟像着了迷似的整天坐在“小人書”攤上。
- 1.&emsp;Li Changkuan, like all children, was originally a pure and adorable child. Before liberation, his father worked on the Nanjing Railway. Because his family was too poor to send him to school, Li Changkuan wandered the streets everyday with petty hooligans, fighting and causing trouble. Whenever he had time, he would read licentious and supernatural xiaorenshu, so mesmerized sitting at the “xiaorenshu” bookstall all day long.
