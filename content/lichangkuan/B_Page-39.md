- 十、&emsp;原來他是這樣打算：想到四川峨嵋山尋師訪道，習演“武藝”和“隱身法術”的，可是在貨倉裏一天沒有吃東西，又渴又餓，心裏又怕，竟大吼大叫起來。船員們發覺問明原由後，又把他送回漢口。
- 10.&emsp;His idea had been to go to Mount Emei and seek out a master to study the dao, to learn “martial arts” and “the magic art of invisibility”. But after a day in the cargo hold without food and drink, he was thirsty, hungry and in fear. Ultimately, he cried out loud. When the boatmen had found him and asked his whereabouts, they sent him back to Hankou.
