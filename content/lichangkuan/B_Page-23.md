- 二十二、&emsp;就這樣混混迷迷的勉強畢業了，但每天仍是想着“掙大錢”“玩女人”，過荒淫生活；所以學業荒蕪了，中學沒考取，於是就偷家裏的錢，賭博和流氓們混在一起談女人。“小人書”不過瘾，還經常看些淫穢的小說，來滿足自己的情慾。
- 22.&emsp;In this muddled and confused state, he barely managed to graduate, but every day, he still thought about “making big money” and “toying with women,” living a life of debauchery;  as a result, he neglected his studies and failed to enter middle school. Then he began stealing money from home, gambling, and mixing with hooligans to talk about women. “Xiaorenshu” no longer satisfied him, and he often read obscene novels to gratify his sexual desires.
