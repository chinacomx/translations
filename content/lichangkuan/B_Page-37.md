- 八、&emsp;回到家裏，父母管教得更嚴了，每天讓他讀書寫字，可是他一點也幹不下去，後來讓他學開汽車，他也不幹，一心贪圖那種荒淫的下流生活，甚至想把他父親殺死，拿家產來供自己揮霍。
- 8.&emsp;When he returned home, his parents disciplined him even more severely. Every day, they had him read and write, but he wouldn’t do any of that. Later, they had him learn to drive a car, but he wouldn’t do that either. With his whole heart, he craved for that licentious life. He even thought about killing his father and then squandering the family property.
