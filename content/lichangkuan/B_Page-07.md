- 六、&emsp;就這樣一天天的過去了。到升入四年級的時候，不少同他一起看“小人書”和聚夥打架的“小侠客”們，在學校的教育與少先隊的辅導下，都慢慢入隊了。星期六，同學們都去過隊日，只剩下他孤零零的一个人；這時，他開始感到孤獨，他想和大夥在一起，他想入隊了。
- 6.&emsp;Days passed like this. When he advanced to the fourth grade, educated by the school and guided by the Young Pioneers, many of the little “swordsmen” who had read “xiaorenshu” and who had ganged up to fight with him gradually joined the Young Pioneers. On Saturdays, classmates all attended team activities, leaving him alone. At this point, he began to feel lonely and he wanted to be with everyone and join the Young Pioneers.
