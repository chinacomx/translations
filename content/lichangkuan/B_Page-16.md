- 十五、&emsp;老師仍舊是那樣耐心地教育他，指出了他錯誤的嚴重性，但他並不理睬。學校通知了家長；父親知道這一系列的事實以後，嚴加斥责，但他感覺学校、家庭都給自己為難，只有王友才順自己的心願。
- 15.&emsp;The teacher still patiently educated him as before, pointing out the seriousness of his mistakes, but he paid no attention. The school notified his parents. After learning about all this, his father sternly reprimanded him. Yet he felt that both the school and his family were making things difficult for him; only Wang Youcai followed his wishes.
