- 十、&emsp;正當他决心做一個好學生、好隊員的時候，却又交上了一個壞蛋朋友，這個“朋友”就是看門的王友才，他原是國民黨和日偽時期的水手、船長，是一個荒淫無恥的大流氓，解放後，改頭換面，混進了學校。經常借講故事為名給孩子們说他過去那腐朽、糜爛的生活，說怎麼“花大堆錢”、怎麼“玩女人”等等，這些東西正引起了李長宽的回憶，使他又沉溺於“小人書”中。
- 10.&emsp;He resolved to be a good student and a good Young Pioneer. However, he also became friends with a bad guy. This “friend” was a doorman, Wang Youcai. At the time of the Guomindang and Japanese puppet government, he had been a sailor and captain and a licentious and shameless hooligan. After Liberation, he changed his appearance and infiltrated the school. Under the pretext of telling stories, he often recounted to the children his past decayed and decadent life, about how he “spent piles of money,” how he “toyed with women,” and so on. These things triggered Li Changkuan’s old memories and caused him to indulge once again in “xiaorenshu.”
