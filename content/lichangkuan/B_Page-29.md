- 二十八、&emsp;现已經過了勞動教育，他瘦弱的身體，漸漸健康起來，思想也同樣健康起來了。他開始悔悟到是什麼使他忘记了自己是工人階級的兒子，又是什麼使他走上了反革命的道路。他每每想到這些，就深惡痛絶地仇恨那荒誕、淫穢的書籍，和像王友才那樣腐朽、糜爛的資產階級思想的毒害。他决心在勞動中改造自己，回到工人階級隊伍裏來。 
- 28.&emsp;Having now gone through education through labor, his frail body had gradually become healthy, and his thoughts had become equally healthy. He began to realize with remorse what had made him forget that he was the son of the working class, and what had misled him onto the counterrevolutionary path. He now often thinks of these issues and feels a deep-seated hatred for those preposterous and obscene books, and for the rotten and degenerate poison of bourgeois ideology the likes of Wang Youcai. He is resolved to reform himself through labor and return among the ranks of the working class.
