- 十七、&emsp;停止學籍期間，更加“無拘無束”，變本加厲起來，直到復學。他的班主任王老師感到不能只用處罰來向他進行教育，便替他還债，給他講述前進的方向和壞書及王友才所說事情的毒害，並且講很多英雄模範故事，對他進行啟發，他慢慢有些轉變了。一次王老師特别給他一本“空軍英雄故事”，讓他仔細閱讀。
- 17.&emsp;During the suspension of his school status, he became all the more “unrestrained,” until he was readmitted. His class teacher, Ms. Wang, felt punishment alone could not educate him, so she paid back his debts, explained to him the right path forward, as well as the poison of the bad books and of what Wang Youcai had told him. She also told many stories of heroic role models to inspire him, and he slowly began to change. Once, Ms. Wang specially gave him a book, _Stories of Air Force Heroes_, and asked him to read it carefully.
