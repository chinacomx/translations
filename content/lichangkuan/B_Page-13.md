- 十二、&emsp;他開始想吃好的、穿好的了，總想着能“發筆大財”，好過王友才和“小人書”中所說的那樣生活，但自己又沒錢，怎麼辦呢？使他起了偷竊的念頭。
- 12.&emsp;He began to crave good food and fine clothes, constantly thinking of “making a big fortune” and living a life like Wang Youcai and the people described in the “xiaorenshu.” But he had no money, what could he do? Thus, he started to think about stealing.
