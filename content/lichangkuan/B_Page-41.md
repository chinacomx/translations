- 十二、&emsp;一天，家裏人都開會去了，留他一個人在家。那荒淫、糜爛的思想，纏繞着他，他趁家中沒人，竟毫無人性地姦污了一個十四歲的幼女。就這樣，一個十八歲的孩子，被關進了牢獄，成了一個姦污幼女，企圖偷越國境的罪犯。
- 12.&emsp;One day, he stayed home alone after everyone had left for a meeting. He was lost in his licentious and decaying thoughts, and using the opportunity that no one was home, he inhumanely raped a young girl of 14 years. That’s how an 18-year-old child became a criminal guilty of raping a young girl and attempting to flee across the border and was thus locked up in prison.

- &emsp;周真森的母親沉痛地說：“反動、淫穢、荒誕的圖書害了我的兒子，使他在年輕的時候就犯了罪！為了再不讓這些壞書繼續毒害更多父母們的兒女，我要求政府一定要把反動、淫穢、荒誕這些殺人不見血的壞書，堅决處理！”
- &emsp;In deep sorrow, Zhou Zhensen’s mother said: “Reactionary, obscene and absurd books have spoilt my son, have made him commit a crime while so young! To prevent these bad books from continuing to poison the sons and daughters of even more mothers and fathers, I demand that the government adamantly dispose of these reactionary, obscene and absurd bad books that kill people without shedding blood!”
