- 十三、&emsp;果然，有一天，同学们都在操场活動，他悄悄蹓進了教室，把一個又白又亮的銅墨盒想装進自己的口袋；但第一次的盜竊行為，引起了他劇烈的思想鬥爭，使他猶豫起來。但為了吃、穿，為了錢，為了能像王友才和“小人書”中的人那樣生活，他終於偷了。從此以後，他偷成了習慣，接二連三，偷鋼筆、偷書本、偷錢……。
- 13.&emsp;Sure enough, one day, while his classmates were all outside on the schoolyard, he quietly slipped into the classroom and tried to put a white and shiny brass ink box into his pocket. But this first attempt of theft caused him a fierce moral conflict, and he hesitated. Yet for the sake of food and clothing, for money, for living like Wang Youcai and the people in the “xiaorenshu,” in the end, he finally stole the box. From then on, stealing became a habit for him, stealing, one after another, fountain pens, books, and money…
