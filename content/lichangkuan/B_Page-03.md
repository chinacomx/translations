- 二、&emsp;可是有時他弄不到一文錢，不能租書看；但“小人書”中的飛仙、劍俠、鏢客、大王的“豪爽之氣”和他們那姦淫婦女的“風流”艷事，緊緊地吸引着他。他只好站在别人的背後閱覽。
- 2.&emsp;But sometimes he could not scrape even a penny to rent books to read. Yet the “bold spirit” of the flying immortals, swordsmen, guards and bandit chiefs and the romantic tales of seducing women in those “xiaorenshu” attracted him closely. He could only read over other people’s shoulders.
