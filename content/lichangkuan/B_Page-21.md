- 二十、&emsp;從此以後，李長寬對王友才有些害怕，總是躲着他；但王友才，一有機會，就侮辱他。時間久了，孩子變樣了，變得黄瘦；王老師問他，他只是羞澀地低下頭去，一語不答。後來終被發覺，王友才，這個禽獸在全校師生的一致控訴和責罵聲中，被逮捕了。
- 20.&emsp;From then on, Li Changkuan became somewhat afraid of Wang Youcai, and always avoided him; but Wang Youcai, whenever he had the chance, molested him. As time passed, the child changed, growing pale and thin; when Ms. Wang asked him about it, he only lowered his head shyly, not saying a word. Later, the truth was finally discovered, and Wang Youcai, this beast, was arrested amid the united accusations and condemnations of the entire school’s teachers and pupils.
