- 十一、&emsp;後來他想：“能‘大堆花錢’、‘玩女人’，不正是“小人書’中所說的‘豪爽’和“風流”吗！？”他簡直聽得入迷，連下課的十分鐘也跑去聽“故事”。壞思想，從此又重新抬了頭。他乾脆連隊日也不過了，整天和王友才偎在一起，老師和同学批評他，他彷彿全沒聽見，只有王友才和“小人書”中所說的“故事”，深深地迷惑着他。
- 11.&emsp;Later, he thought: “Being able to ‘spend piles of money’ and ‘toy with women’, isn’t that exactly what the ‘xiaorenshu’ call being ‘bold and generous’ and ‘romantic’?” He was utterly fascinated, even rushing to listen to these “stories” during the ten-minute breaks between classes. Bad thoughts, from then on, rose up again. He even stopped participating in the Young Pioneer Days and spent all his time huddled together with Wang Youcai. When teachers and classmates criticized him, he acted as if he had heard nothing at all. Only Wang Youcai and the “stories” described in the “xiaorenshu” deeply bewitched him.
