- 二十一、&emsp;王友才雖被逮捕，但他散佈的毒素，却在李長寬的心裏生下了根子，淫亂無稽的念頭，愈來愈加嚴重；他每天神情恍惚，想入非非。課堂上不能靜心聽講，竟想像王友才和“小人書”中的人物一樣的去侮辱女同學。
- 21.&emsp;Although Wang Youcai was arrested, the poison he had spread had taken root in Li Changkuan’s heart, and his obscene and irrational thoughts grew more and more serious; every day he seemed absent-minded, lost in wild fantasies. In class he could no longer concentrate on the lessons, and even imagined molesting his female classmates like Wang Youcai and the characters in the “xiaorenshu” had done.
